Benchmarking Caveats & Methodological Notes

1.

Relevance labeling: Gold annotations are binary relevance judgments established by two independent aerospace domain engineers; graded multi-level relevance (0-3) is under review for Suite v2.5.

2.

Embedding domain shift: Dense retriever text-embedding-3-small was not fine-tuned on aerospace text, causing mild vocabulary mismatch on rare NASA acronyms (e.g. SMA, SE&I, KDP).

3.

RFF Fusion Constant: The parameter k=60 was chosen a priori based on Cormack et al. (2009). Grid sweep across k ∈ [20, 100] shows flat optima between 50 and 70 (Run #14).

4.

Bootstrap resampling: 95% Confidence Intervals are non-parametric percentile bounds calculated from B = 10,000 bootstrap replicates sampled with replacement.
